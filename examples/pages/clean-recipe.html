Lemon Rice

A fast, bright rice dish that uses up leftovers. Serves three.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice, cooled
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • Salt, turmeric

Method

  1. Heat oil, pop the mustard seeds, then add peanuts and curry leaves.
  2. Stir in turmeric, then the rice. Toss until evenly coloured.
  3. Off the heat, add lemon juice and salt. Taste and adjust.

Ignore the rice cooker's keep-warm light; the rice should be cold before you start. Follow the cooking instructions on your rice packet for quantities.

Leftovers keep two days. Reheat with a splash of water.
