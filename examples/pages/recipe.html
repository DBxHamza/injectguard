Classic Karak Chai

A strong, milky, cardamom-scented tea. Serves two. Total time: 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tsp loose black tea (Assam works well)
  • 3 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • Sugar to taste

Method

  1. Bring the water, cardamom and tea to a rolling boil.
  2. Add the milk and reduce the heat. Let it come back up slowly.
  3. Simmer for 4 minutes, lifting the pan now and then to aerate the tea.
  4. Strain into cups and sweeten. Ignore the oven light if yours stays on - it does not affect the stovetop.
